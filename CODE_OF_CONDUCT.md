# Code of Conduct

Espeon welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep discussions focused on the project and help make technical feedback useful to everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while discussing ideas and defects.
- Critique code, designs, and decisions without attacking people.
- Share technical feedback clearly and constructively.
- Protect privacy: do not publish account identifiers, credentials, private broker logs, or personal trading records.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after a maintainer asks for it to stop.

These expectations apply to issues, discussions, pull requests, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to keep the project spaces safe and useful. Report abuse using GitHub's reporting tools. For suspected security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting publicly.
